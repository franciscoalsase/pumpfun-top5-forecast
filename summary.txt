World news digest: Wednesday 11 November 2026 (source in brackets; unconfirmed claims flagged)

WAR AND DIPLOMACY
- Ukraine: US-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi ended after two days with an agreement in principle on a large prisoner exchange but no ceasefire or territorial deal [AP, Reuters].
- Gulf: Saudi Arabia said it intercepted seven Houthi missiles and drones; a Houthi claim of hitting a Yanbu oil station is unconfirmed. Iran's Mohsen Rezaei said no enriched nuclear material will leave the country. Brent rose 1.39% to $92.76 [Reuters].
- Gaza: Israeli fire killed at least seven, including a father and his son, 13; the army said it hit militants [Reuters]. Israel's new Knesset was sworn in [AP].
- NATO: at least four stray drones entered Baltic airspace and jets scrambled [Reuters]. UK police arrested a US soldier suspected of spying for Russia [BBC].
